    Draw the secant line passing through the points \((-2,2)\) and \((1,-1)\text{.}\) What is its slope?
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(m=-1\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (b)
    Draw the tangent line passing through the point \((1,-1)\text{.}\) What is its slope, approximately?
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(f'(1) \approx -1\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (c)
    Why do you think these two values are similar?
    🔗
    Hint.
    Think about the definition of the slope of the secant line between two points, \((a,f(a))\) and \((a+h,f(a+h))\text{,}\) separated by \(h\) units. Compare this to the definition of the slope of the tangent line between two points.
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer.
    For relatively small values of \(h\text{,}\) in this case \(h=3\text{,}\) \(f'(1) = \displaystyle\lim_{h\rightarrow 0}\dfrac{f(1+h)-f(1)}{h} \approx \dfrac{f(1+h)-f(1)}{h} = \dfrac{f(4)-f(1)}{3} = -1\text{.}\)

    6.

    Complete the following tasks to find the instantaneous rate of change of \(f(x)=x^2+2x-1\) at \(x=1\text{.}\)
    🔗
    (a)
    Compute \(f(1+h)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(f(1+h)=h^2+4h+2\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (b)
    Compute \(f(1+h)-f(1)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(f(1+h)=(h^2+4h+2)-(1^2+2-1)=h^2+4h\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (c)
    Compute \(\dfrac{f(1+h)-f(1)}{h}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\dfrac{f(1+h)-f(1)}{h} = \dfrac{h^2+4h}{h} = h+4\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (d)
    Compute \(\displaystyle \lim_{h\rightarrow 0}\dfrac{f(1+h)-f(1)}{h}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\displaystyle \lim_{h\rightarrow 0}\dfrac{f(1+h)-f(1)}{h} = \lim_{h\rightarrow 0}(h+4)=4\)
    🔗
    🔗
