    Getting Warmed Up and in the Right Mindset.

    1.

    Let \(f(x)\) and \(g(x)\) be function and suppose that \(\displaystyle \lim_{x\rightarrow a}f(x)\) and \(\displaystyle \lim_{x\rightarrow a}g(x)\) exists and are finite. Choose either TRUE or FALSE for each of the following statements. Keep in mind potential counterexamples.
    🔗
    1. \(\displaystyle \displaystyle \lim_{x\rightarrow a}[6f(x)-2g(x)] = 6\lim_{x\rightarrow a}f(x) - 2\lim_{x\rightarrow a}g(x)\)
      🔗
      🔗
    2. \(\displaystyle \displaystyle \lim_{x\rightarrow a}[f(x)g(x)] = \left(\lim_{x\rightarrow a}f(x)\right)\left(\lim_{x\rightarrow a}g(x)\right)\)
      🔗
      🔗
    3. \(\displaystyle \displaystyle \lim_{x\rightarrow a}\dfrac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \dfrac{\displaystyle \lim_{x\rightarrow a}f(x)}{\displaystyle \lim_{x\rightarrow a}g(x)}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    4. \(\displaystyle \displaystyle \lim_{x\rightarrow a}\sqrt[4]{f(x)} = \sqrt[4]{\lim_{x\rightarrow a}f(x)}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    5. \(\displaystyle \displaystyle \lim_{x\rightarrow a}\ln(f(x)) = \ln\left(\lim_{x\rightarrow a}f(x)\right)\)
      🔗
      🔗
    6. \(\displaystyle \displaystyle \lim_{x\rightarrow a}\arctan(f(x)) = \arctan\left(\lim_{x\rightarrow a}f(x)\right)\)
      🔗
      🔗
    7. \(\displaystyle \displaystyle \lim_{x\rightarrow a}e^{f(x)} = e^{\left(\displaystyle \lim_{x\rightarrow a}f(x)\right)}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    Answer.
    (a)-T, (b)-T, (c)-F, (d)-F, (e)-T, (f)-T, (g)-T
