    Getting Warmed Up and in the Right Mindset.

    1.

    Consider vectors \(\mathbf{a}=2\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}\) and \(\mathbf{b}=-\mathbf{i}+4\mathbf{j}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    (a)
    Find the scalar (dot) product between \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{b}\text{,}\) \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=-14\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (b)
    Find the scalar projection of \(\mathbf{b}\) onto \(\mathbf{a}\text{,}\) \(\text{comp}_{\mathbf{a}}(\mathbf{b})\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\comp_{\mathbf{a}}(\mathbf{(b)})=\dfrac{-14\sqrt{13}}{13}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (c)
    Find the orthogonal, or vector, projection of \(\mathbf{b}\) onto \(\mathbf{a}\text{,}\) \(\text{proj}_{\mathbf{a}}(\mathbf{b})\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\text{proj}_{\mathbf{a}}(\mathbf{b})=\left\langle -\dfrac{28}{13},\dfrac{42}{13}\right\rangle\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (d)
    Find the angle between \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{b}\text{,}\) \(\theta\text{.}\) You may leave your answer in terms of inverse trigonometric functions, if applicable.
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\theta=\arccos\left(-\dfrac{14}{\sqrt{13}\sqrt{17}}\right)\)

    4.

    Find the derivative \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) for each of the following functions. You may use derivative rules for this exercise.
    🔗
    (a)
    \(y=\sqrt[5]{2-9\sec(x)}\left(x^3-2\cot(x)\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{1}{5}(2-9\sec(x))(-9\sec(x)\tan(x))\left(x^3-2\cot(x)\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}}-\dfrac{2}{3}\sqrt[5]{2-9\sec(x)}\left(x^3-2\cot(x)\right)^{-\frac{5}{3}}\left(3x^2+2\csc^2(x)\right)\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (b)
    \(y=-\dfrac{\csc(2x)}{5^{x^2}}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=-\dfrac{5^{x^2}(-2\csc(2x)\cot(2x))-\csc(2x)5^{x^2}\ln(5)(2x)}{\left(5^{x^2}\right)^2}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (c)
    \(y=\sin(\ln(\arcsin(-x)))\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\cos(\ln(\arcsin(-x)))\left(\dfrac{1}{\arcsin(-x)}\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\right)(-1)\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (d)
    \(y=\begin{cases}\log_7(-\pi x) \amp x \lt 0 \\ e^x\cos\left(\sqrt{x}\right) \amp x \geq 0 \end{cases}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(y=\begin{cases}\dfrac{1}{x\ln(7)} \amp x \lt 0 \\ e^x\cos\left(\sqrt{x}\right)-e^x\dfrac{\sin\left(\sqrt{x}\right)}{2\sqrt{x}} \amp x \gt 0 \end{cases}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (e)
    \(y=(\tan(x))^{\arctan(x)}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=(\tan(x))^{\arctan(x)}\left(\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}\ln(\tan(x))+\arctan(x)\dfrac{\sec^2(x)}{\tan(x)}\right)\)

    🔗
