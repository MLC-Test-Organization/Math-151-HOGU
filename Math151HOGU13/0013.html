    Getting Warmed Up and in the Right Mindset.

    1.

    Consider the function \(f(x)=x^4-4x^3\text{.}\)
    🔗
    (a)
    Determine the critical numbers of \(f\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(x=0,3\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (b)
    Determine the \(x\) and \(y\)-coordinates of any local extrema of \(f\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(x=3, y=-27\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (c)
    Determine the interval(s) where \(f\) is/are increasing and the interval(s) where \(f\) is/are decreasing
    🔗
    Answer.
    Increasing on \((3,\infty)\text{,}\) decreasing on \((-\infty,0)\cup(0,3)\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (d)
    Determine the interval(s) where \(f\) is/are concave up and the interval(s) where \(f\) is/are concave down.
    🔗
    Answer.
    Concave up on \((-\infty,0)\cup(2,\infty)\text{,}\) concave down on \((0,2)\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (e)
    Determine the \(x\) and \(y\)-coordinates of any inflection points of \(f\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \((0,0)\) and \((2,-16)\)
    🔗
    🔗

    1.

    Find the equations of both tangent lines to the parabola \(f(x)=x^2+3x+8\) which pass through the point \((0,4)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(y-18=7(x-2)\) and \(y-6=-(x+2)\) OR \(y=7x+4\) and \(y=-x+4\)

    Water is being drained from an inverted conical tank with a radius of \(20\)m and a height of \(12\)m. If the water level is falling at a rate of \(\dfrac{3}{10}\frac{\text{m}}{\text{min}}\) when the water level is \(3\)m from the bottom of the tank, at what rate is the water being drained from the tank.

    3.

    The acceleration vector of a particle, in meters and as a function of time \(t\geq 0\text{,}\) is modelled by the vector function \(\mathbf{a}(t)=\langle 6t,4\sqrt{t}\rangle\text{.}\)
    🔗
    (a)
    Determine the velocity vector \(\mathbf{v}(t)\) if \(\mathbf{v}(0)=\langle -3,2\rangle\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\mathbf{v}(t)=\left\langle 3t^2-3,\dfrac{8}{3}t^{\frac{3}{2}}+2 \right\rangle\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (b)
    Determine the position vector \(\mathbf{s}(t)\) if \(\mathbf{s}(0)=\langle 1,4 \rangle\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\mathbf{s}(t)=\left\langle t^3-3t-1, \dfrac{16}{15}t^{\frac{5}{2}}+2t-4 \right\rangle\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (c)
    At what time(s), if any, is the particle stationary?
    🔗
    Answer.
    The particle is never stationary.

    4.

    An open top box with a square base is to be constructed from a material that costs \(8\) cents per \(\text{in}^2\text{.}\) Given that the volume of the box is to be \(2744\text{in}^3\text{,}\) find the dimensions of the box that minimize the cost of the box. What is the minimum cost of the box? Show that your answer is a minimum using any of the methods you’ve learned in class.
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(L=W=14\sqrt[3]{2}\)in, \(H=\dfrac{14}{\sqrt[3]{4}}\)in, the minimum cost is \(\dfrac{32\cdot2744}{14\sqrt[3]{2}}\approx 7467\) cents or \(\$74.67\text{.}\) You may show that your answer is a local minimum using the first or second derivative tests on the cost objective function.
