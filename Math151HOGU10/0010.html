      🔗

      🔗
      🔗
    4. To find the displacement of a particle at time \(t\text{,}\) we find the net area under the velocity vs. time curve from time \(0\) to time \(t\text{.}\)
      🔗

    5. To find the total distance traveled by a particle at time \(t\text{,}\) we find the net area under the velocity vs. time curve from time \(0\) to time \(t\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    6. The formula for a Right Riemann sum of a function \(f(x)\) on the interval \([a,b]\) is given by \(\displaystyle R_n=\sum_{i=1}^{n}f\left(x_i^*\right)\Delta x = \sum_{i=1}^{n}f\left(a+i\left(\dfrac{b-a}{n}\right)\right)\left(\dfrac{b-a}{n}\right)\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    7. Assuming \(a \lt x\text{,}\) the derivative of the antiderivative of a function \(f(x)\) on the interval \([a,x]\) yields the original function \(f(x)\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    8. Assuming \(a \lt x\text{,}\) the antiderivative of the derivative of a function \(f(x)\) on the interval \([a,x]\) yields the original function \(f(x)\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗

    a-FALSE, b-TRUE, c-FALSE, d-TRUE, e-FALSE, f-TRUE, g-TRUE, h-FALSE
