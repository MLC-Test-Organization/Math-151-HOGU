    Getting Warmed Up and in the Right Mindset.

    1.

    All of the integrals below can be evaluated using \(u\)-substitution. Identify the correct choice of \(u\) and \(du\) for the substitution rule in each of the following integrals.
    🔗
    (a)
    \(\displaystyle \int\left(3x-1\right)^{10}\ dx\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(u=3x-1\text{,}\) \(du=3\ dx\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (b)
    \(\displaystyle \int\sqrt{5x-7}\ dx\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(u=5x-7\text{,}\) \(du=5\ dx\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (c)
    \(\displaystyle \int\dfrac{1}{2x+3}\ dx\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(u=2x+3\text{,}\) \(du=2\ dx\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (d)
    \(\displaystyle \int 4xe^{x^2-3}\ dx\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(u=x^2-3\text{,}\) \(du=2x\ dx\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (e)
    \(\displaystyle \int 6x\sin\left(3x^2\right)\ dx\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(u=3x^2\text{,}\) \(du=6x\ dx\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (f)
    \(\displaystyle \int \sin^2(x)\cos(x)\ dx\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(u=\sin(x)\text{,}\) \(du=\cos(x)\ dx\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (g)
    \(\displaystyle \int \left(x^2-2x\right)\ln\left(x^3-3x^2\right)\ dx\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(y=x^3-3x^2\text{,}\) \(du=3x^2-6x\ dx\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (h)
    \(\displaystyle \int \dfrac{3x}{\sqrt{9-x^2}}\ dx\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(u=9-x^2\text{,}\) \(du=-2x\ dx\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Let Us Get Our Hands On.

    1.

    Use FTC1 to evaluate the following expressions. If FTC1 doesn’t apply, then state so.
    🔗
    (a)
    \(\displaystyle \dfrac{d}{dx}\int_{1}^{x}\ln(t)\ dt\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(\ln(x)\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    (b)
    \(\displaystyle \dfrac{d}{dx}\int_{-1}^{x}\sqrt{t-5}\ dt\)
    🔗
    Answer.
    FTC1 does not apply since \(\sqrt{t-5}\) is not continuous on \((-1,x)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    \(\displaystyle \dfrac{d}{dx}\int_{-2}^{x}\tan(t)\ dt\)
